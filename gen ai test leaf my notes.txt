Chain of Thought prompting is a technique where we ask AI to solve a problem step by step instead of directly giving the final answer.
We break a complex problem into smaller steps so that AI can provide a clear and structured explanation.
For example, instead of asking AI for only the answer, we can ask it to explain the solution step by step.
This is useful for solving complex problems and understanding the solution.

Socratic Prompting is a technique where we ask AI to guide us through questions instead of directly giving answers.
AI asks questions one by one to encourage critical thinking and help us find the answer ourselves.
It is useful for learning, problem-solving, and understanding concepts.
